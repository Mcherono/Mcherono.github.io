01 — About

A bit about me

My background is a bit unusual. I spent four years studying actuarial science — so before I ever wrote a line of Python, I was already being trained to think carefully about what numbers actually mean, where they come from, and when you shouldn't trust them.

Then I did an MSc in Data Science at AIMS Rwanda, graduating with a Distinction. The combination has been useful. Most of the time, the hard part isn't the model — it's understanding the data well enough to know what question you're actually answering.

I've worked in insurance, wholesale, and research. The data is always different. The problem is usually the same: something important is buried in it, and someone needs to know what it is. That's the part I enjoy.
