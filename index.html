01 — About

Where Actuarial Rigour
Meets Data Science

I am a data science professional with a distinctive combination of actuarial training, advanced statistical methods, and hands-on industry experience in insurance analytics. My journey has been defined by a commitment to turning messy, real-world data into clear, actionable intelligence.

Holding an MSc in Mathematical Sciences (Data Science) with Distinction from AIMS Rwanda and a First Class Honours BSc in Actuarial Science from Karatina University, I bring quantitative depth that goes beyond standard analytics. I am also partially through my CIFA qualification, adding financial and investment analysis acumen to my profile.

I have delivered analytics solutions in insurance underwriting, risk analysis, and compliance at Britam Insurance and Minet Kenya — environments where precision is non-negotiable. I now apply that same rigour to data pipelines, automated reporting, and machine learning projects.

I am actively seeking Data Analyst, Business Analyst, and Data Scientist roles — remote or on-site — globally, particularly where African market expertise and cross-domain analytical thinking create real value.
