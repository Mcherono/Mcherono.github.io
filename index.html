01 — About

Where Actuarial Rigour
Meets Data Science

My background is a bit unusual — I studied actuarial science for my undergrad, then went on to do an MSc in Data Science at AIMS Rwanda, which I completed with a Distinction. Along the way I also worked in insurance at Britam and Minet Kenya, so I understand how data actually gets used in business settings.

I work mainly in Python and R, and I enjoy the full process — cleaning messy data, building pipelines, running analysis, and presenting findings in a way that actually makes sense to people. I am also partially through my CIFA qualification, which has given me a solid grounding in finance and investment.

Right now I am doing a data science internship with Future Interns and working on a side project called Auto Analytics Studio — a tool that uses AI to automate the analysis and reporting process from any dataset. It is something I am building because I think it is genuinely useful.

I am looking for Data Analyst, Business Analyst, or Data Scientist roles — remote or on-site. I am based in Kigali but open to opportunities anywhere.
